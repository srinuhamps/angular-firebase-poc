About Carolle Hair Studio

A sanctuary for beauty, artistry, and authentic hair care.

Carolle Hair Studio was founded with a single, powerful vision: to create a space where every woman feels celebrated. A place where hair care isn't just a service — it's an experience.

After over a decade of perfecting the art of protective styling, founder Carolle Mensah opened her studio doors in Atlanta and quickly became the city's most sought-after hair destination.

What started as a one-chair studio has grown into a full luxury salon with an in-house beauty supply section, a team of expert stylists, and thousands of happy clients.

"Hair is the crown you never take off. It deserves to be treated like royalty."

— Carolle Mensah, Founder
